This Privacy Policy explains how [[LEGAL_ENTITY]], doing business as **Texas
Tree Tops** ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information you
provide when you contact us or request an estimate for tree services in West
Dallas, TX and the surrounding communities we serve.

## Information we collect

When you submit a form on our website or contact us, we may collect:

- **Contact details** you provide: your name, phone number, and email address.
- **Service details** you choose to share about the work you need.
- **Advertising and analytics data** collected automatically when you arrive
  from an online ad or visit our pages: a click identifier (such as a Google
  click ID), the campaign that referred you, your approximate page activity,
  and standard technical data (browser type, device, and a timestamp). We use
  Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager and call-tracking for this purpose.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## How we use your information

- To respond to your request, schedule an estimate, and provide tree services.
- To contact you about your request by phone, text message, or email.
- To measure and improve our advertising, so we understand which ads lead to
  genuine service requests.
- To keep records required for our business and to comply with law.

## SMS / text messaging (A2P consent)

If you check the SMS consent box on our form, you agree to receive text
messages from Texas Tree Tops about your estimate and service. Message
frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out
at any time, or **HELP** for help.

**We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or your SMS consent with any
third party or affiliate for their own marketing or promotional purposes.**
Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.
Consenting to text messages is not a condition of purchasing any service.

## How we share information

We share information only as needed to run our business:

- **Service providers** who act on our behalf — for example our customer
  relationship management (CRM) system, our text-messaging and call-tracking
  providers, and our advertising and analytics providers — and only so they can
  perform services for us.
- **Legal reasons** — if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or
  property.

We do not sell your personal information. As stated above, mobile opt-in and
consent data is never shared with third parties.

## Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide services and to meet
our legal and business obligations, then delete or de-identify it.

## Your choices

- **Text messages:** reply STOP to opt out at any time.
- **Access or deletion:** contact us at [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] to request a copy of,
  correction to, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. If

## Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the
information you provide. No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
