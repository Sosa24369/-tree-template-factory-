## Why this is new copy rather than a reproduction

The live storm page is ~69% identical to the removal page. About a third of it is
genuinely storm copy; the rest is un-edited removal boilerplate, including the $300
summer-removal offer, West Dallas geography on a Fort Worth page, and a removal FAQ.
Reproducing that would give you a "storm control" that is mostly a removal page.

So: everything below is written for storm. Where the source already had real storm
copy that works, I have kept its substance and tightened only the delivery — those
lines are marked **[from source]**. Everything else is new.

**Geography:** Fort Worth and Tarrant County (what the source storm page targets).
**Offer:** free on-site storm assessment with written damage documentation — not a
discount. Discounts are wrong for emergency intent; availability and speed are the
currency.

## Claims check

Every claim below is one the source pages already make, or is a process statement.
Nothing invents a credential, a response-time guarantee, or an insurance outcome.

- "Licensed and insured, up to $2 million" — stated on the source pages.
- Insurance language deliberately stops at documentation. It never says or implies a
  claim will be approved. **[from source]**: "Coverage decisions are between you and
  your carrier."
- The 911 line is kept verbatim in substance — it is a safety instruction and should
  not be softened for tone.
- No response-time promise is stated as a guarantee. "Same-day assessment in most
  cases" mirrors the source's hedged wording. **If you want a hard promise ("we
  answer within 60 minutes"), tell me and I will put it in — I will not invent one.**

## Three things to decide

1. **Response-time promise.** I have used "same-day assessment in most cases", which
   is the source's hedged wording. A hard promise converts better on emergency
   intent — but only you can commit the crew to it.
2. **After-hours.** FAQ 8 implies you take night and weekend calls. If that isn't
   true, say so and I'll cut it.
3. **Offer.** I've made the offer "free assessment + documentation" rather than a
   discount, because discounting reads wrong when someone's roof is open. If you want
   a storm-specific discount instead, tell me the number.
